Fresh graduate resume guide

Resume guide for Singapore fresh graduates: show proof, not just potential.

A fresh graduate resume should help a recruiter see direction, evidence, and fit quickly. The goal is to turn ordinary student experience into employer language without exaggerating.

See before and after proof Request resume review

Resume structure

What to include when experience is still limited.

Sections that usually matter

  • Clear profile summary matched to the target role.
  • Education with relevant modules only when they support the role.
  • Internship, project, CCA, competition, or part-time work evidence.
  • Skills grouped by tools, analysis, communication, and domain knowledge.
  • Leadership or service examples where they show responsibility.

Common mistakes

  • Sending the same generic resume to every opening.
  • Listing tasks without action, context, or result.
  • Using too much school language and too little employer language.
  • Leaving LinkedIn empty or inconsistent with the resume.
  • Waiting until the interview invite to prepare examples.

Rewrite method

Convert every weak bullet into role-relevant evidence.

A stronger bullet usually names the action, context, tool, stakeholder, and outcome. Even a school project can become useful evidence when written in the language of the target job.

Weak Generic wording
  • Did research for group project.
  • Helped customers in part-time job.
  • Used Excel for reports.
Stronger Recruiter-readable wording
  • Researched customer segments, competitors, and pricing options for a go-to-market project presentation.
  • Handled customer enquiries during peak shifts, escalating issues and maintaining service accuracy.
  • Used Excel to clean data, update weekly tracking, and summarize findings for team review.
