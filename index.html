Graduate Industry Traineeships (GRIT) Programme

Theme: Traineeship pathways | Date checked: 23 Jul 2026

Why this matters

Not every graduate lands a full-time role immediately. GRIT gives eligible fresh graduates a structured 3- to 6-month way to build industry experience, earn an allowance, and improve their conversion chances when the market is slower.

What to understand

Treat a traineeship like a proof-building bridge: choose one where the daily work creates resume bullets, supervisor references, and clearer evidence for your next full-time application.
