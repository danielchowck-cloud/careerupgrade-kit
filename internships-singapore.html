What to prepare before applying

  • A one-page resume matched to the internship type.
  • Three project or CCA examples that show practical evidence.
  • A LinkedIn headline that says more than student or undergraduate.
  • Two short interview stories on teamwork, initiative, or problem solving.

Application focus

Internship resumes should show evidence, not only modules.

Employers know students may not have full-time experience. Strong applications translate school projects, part-time work, competitions, and CCAs into the same skills used in the internship.

1

Pick a role family

Choose one main direction such as analytics, marketing, finance, engineering, HR, or operations.

2

Match your proof

Select projects, tools, coursework, and work examples that fit that role family.

3

Apply with context

Use a targeted resume and short interview stories before clicking apply.
