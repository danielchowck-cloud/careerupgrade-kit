Agent-operated service model

Every paid student should move through a clear service workflow.

This is the operating layer behind CareerUpgrade Kit: research, product, growth, support, finance, and analytics working from the same weekly plan.

CEO

Decide the weekly target.

Pick one growth priority, one product improvement, and one customer proof point to chase.

Research

Refresh the opportunity map.

Keep Singapore internship and first-job examples current by stage, industry, role, and source.

Product

Tighten the paid journey.

Improve the dashboard, intake questions, resume outputs, and interview preparation pack.

Growth

Turn market signals into content.

Create short career reads, parent/student share copy, and landing-page tests from live hiring patterns.

Support

Protect response quality.

Track leads, draft follow-ups, close inactive cases, and keep every customer next step visible.

Finance

Watch payment health.

Check Stripe flow, package pricing, email confirmation, refund risk, and paid-to-delivered status.

Reusable launch workflow

The same operating system can launch future projects faster.

01

Validate

  • Define customer pain and buying trigger.
  • Check live search demand and competitor offers.
  • Write the smallest paid offer worth testing.
02

Launch

  • Build the landing page, payment path, and request form.
  • Add analytics, source tracking, and follow-up capture.
  • Publish a simple dashboard or delivery workspace.
03

Operate

  • Run daily health, lead, payment, and source checks.
  • Refresh content from current market data.
  • Escalate only meaningful customer or revenue signals.
04

Improve

  • Review conversion friction and customer questions weekly.
  • Turn delivered work into proof, templates, and examples.
  • Kill, pause, or scale based on actual demand.
