Sample 01

Business student applying for sales or business development roles.

Before

Student wording

  • Helped the sales team with admin work.
  • Prepared reports and contacted customers.
  • Did a school project about launching a product.
After

Hiring-manager wording

  • Supported customer follow-up tracking, quotation updates, and weekly sales reporting for a B2B sales team.
  • Prepared competitor and pricing summaries to help the team compare market positioning.
  • Built a go-to-market project covering customer segment, pricing angle, competitor landscape, and presentation recommendations.
Why stronger

The revised version shows function, context, tools, and business outcome. It gives a recruiter faster evidence for sales coordination, business development, and customer-facing graduate roles.

Sample 02

Engineering student applying for process, manufacturing, or operations roles.

Before

Student wording

  • Worked on a group engineering project.
  • Did testing and wrote a report.
  • Helped troubleshoot problems in the lab.
After

Hiring-manager wording

  • Completed a team-based prototype project involving test planning, measurement checks, and issue documentation.
  • Analysed test results to identify likely causes of performance variation and proposed corrective actions.
  • Maintained structured lab notes covering setup, observations, failed attempts, and final recommendations.
Why stronger

The revised version signals engineering discipline: testing, documentation, root-cause thinking, and reliability. That is more useful than simply saying the student joined a project.

Sample 03

Data or analytics student applying for internship roles.

Before

Student wording

  • Used Excel and Python for data analysis.
  • Made charts for a class project.
  • Presented findings to classmates.
After

Hiring-manager wording

  • Cleaned and analysed a project dataset using Excel and Python to identify patterns, outliers, and segment-level differences.
  • Created visual summaries to explain key drivers behind the data instead of presenting charts without interpretation.
  • Presented findings with recommended next steps, limitations, and follow-up questions for deeper analysis.
Why stronger

The upgraded bullets show analytical thinking, not just software exposure. This helps a recruiter see judgment, interpretation, and communication.
